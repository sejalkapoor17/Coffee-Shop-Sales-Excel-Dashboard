# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard transforms transactional sales data into actionable business insights by analyzing revenue, product performance, sales trends, and customer purchasing patterns.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Coffee shop management needs to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and peak sales periods to make better decisions related to inventory, staffing, promotions, and product strategy.

## 📊 Key KPIs

- 💰 Total Revenue
- 🧾 Total Transactions
- 🛍️ Units Sold
- 💵 Average Order Value
- 📅 Monthly Sales
- ☕ Product-wise Sales
- 📦 Category-wise Sales
- ⏰ Peak Sales Periods

## 📈 Key Insights

- Identified the best-performing products based on sales and revenue.
- Analyzed monthly sales trends to identify high- and low-performing periods.
- Evaluated product and category performance to understand revenue contribution.
- Identified customer purchasing patterns and peak sales periods.
- Highlighted opportunities for inventory planning and promotional strategies.

## 💼 Business Value

The dashboard helps coffee shop management monitor sales performance, identify high-demand products, optimize inventory and staffing, and make data-driven business decisions.
